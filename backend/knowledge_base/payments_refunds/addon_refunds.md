# Payments & Refunds: Add-On Billing and Refund Policy

Add-on services include:
- Bestseller Package
- PR Campaign
- Literary Award Submission
- Social Media Promotion
- Fast Track Publishing

Billing model:
- charges are applied at activation
- recurring charges apply where listed in package terms

Refund rules:
- completed services are non-refundable
- processing services may be partially refundable based on delivery stage
- pending services may be cancelled with full reversal within policy window

Escalate billing disputes when:
- invoice and dashboard records mismatch
- service status is delayed beyond SLA
- duplicate billing is reported
