# Payments & Refunds: Royalty Payment Process

Royalty payments are processed quarterly.

Authors receive royalty statements between the 5th and 10th of the month following quarter close.

Payout details:
- transferred to registered bank account
- minimum payout threshold: INR 1000
- on-hold status may occur for incomplete tax or bank details

If payments are delayed:
1. verify royalty status in dashboard
2. confirm bank profile details
3. contact support for payout audit

Support should clearly differentiate between pending, processing, paid, and on-hold states.
