# Publishing & Distribution: Author Copy Delivery and Tracking

Author copies are dispatched within 5 to 10 business days after live listing.

Tracking process:
- dispatch status updated in dashboard
- courier details shared over email
- delays flagged during high-demand periods

Escalate copy-related issues when:
- tracking is unavailable after dispatch window
- delivery is delayed beyond carrier SLA
- shipment is marked lost or returned
